How the figures are calculated

Paid occupancy = paid room nights ÷ available room nights. ARR / ADR = room income excluding tax ÷ paid room nights. RevPAR = room income ÷ available room nights. TRevPAR uses all operating income. Complimentary rooms are included in physical occupancy, but excluded from paid room nights.

Dates use India time. Checkout day is excluded. Booked performance includes confirmed future stays; Actual includes only stays marked checked in or checked out, through yesterday. Unverified past stays need owner review. Website-only closures still count as available capacity; only rooms explicitly marked out of order are removed.

Full-stay income, tax and selling costs are spread evenly across stay nights. Operating entries are spread evenly across their service dates. These are management estimates where nightly rates differ. Revenue is separate from cash received. Collections show current whole-stay amounts for selected reservations and are not a cash-flow report.

Lead time uses the original booking date. Direct share includes website, direct, phone / WhatsApp and walk-in stays; Other remains separate. Cancellation and no-show rates use reservations due to arrive in the period. Abandoned payment holds are excluded. Retained cancellation charges must be entered as other operating income.

Profit is an operating result based on entered income, commission, gateway fees and operating costs. It excludes financing, depreciation and income tax. It remains provisional until all bookings and costs are reviewed. Revenue marked — needs missing data; known allocations are shown separately. Current bookings reflect amendments and cancellations as they stand now.
